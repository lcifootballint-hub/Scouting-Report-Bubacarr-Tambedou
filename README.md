# Player Scouting & Performance Analysis: Bubacarr Tambedou

**LCIFootballInt | Individual Player Scouting Report**

This project represents my first step in developing my skills as a football analyst, player scout, and recruitment analyst.

I have analysed Bubacarr Tambedou, a Gambian centre-forward playing for Sønderjyske in the Danish Superliga. My assessment combines video based observations with statistical research and data visualisations to develop a more informed understanding of his performance, playing style, strengths, and potential areas for development.

### Methodology

- **Video analysis:** Reviewed three full match videos and additional short video clips to assess his movements, positioning, attacking behaviour, decision making, and contributions as a **centre forward**.
- **Statistical research:** Collected and examined available player performance data to identify relevant trends and insights.
- **Data visualisation:** Created visual representations of selected performance metrics to communicate findings clearly and support the scouting assessment.
- **Scouting assessment:** Combined qualitative observations and quantitative evidence to develop an initial evaluation of the player.

### Objective

The objective is to understand how video analysis and performance data can complement each other in player evaluation and recruitment. I believe Tambedou has qualities worth investigating further, and this report documents my current assessment rather than presenting a definitive prediction of his future career.

### A Note on This Project

This is my first individual player scouting report as I develop my knowledge and practical experience in football analytics and recruitment. I recognise that a small sample of matches has limitations, and my conclusions may evolve as I review more footage, collect additional data, and improve my analytical methods.

This project is part of my ongoing journey with **LCIFootballInt**, where I aim to combine football knowledge, data analysis, and structured scouting to produce evidence-based player assessments.
